Features

Built to feel the music

Trippin doesn't loop pretty clips. It listens, works out where the music is going, and moves with it.

Listening

It knows the song

Band levels, onsets and kicks are tracked live, along with tempo and the beat and bar phase. A neural downbeat check keeps the "one" on the one, even in house music where off-beat hats try to steal the grid.

  • Four bands of loudness, transients and slow presence drive every scene.
  • Breakdowns detected. When the drums drop out, the camera slows, flashes stop and the visuals soften.
  • Level independent. It works the same at a quiet bedroom volume or a loud club system.
Motion, not flashing

The music changes the shape

Brightness is the least interesting thing a visual can do. In Trippin the music reshapes objects, swings the camera with each phrase, and drives speed and complexity as a track builds.

  • Shape: morphs, displacement and geometry follow the sound.
  • Direction: motion reverses and swings over phrases.
  • Energy: travel speeds up and surges on drops, always smoothly.
Timeline editor + AI

Script a whole set, or let AI direct it

Load a track and Trippin analyses it offline: waveform, beat and bar grid anchored to the real downbeat, and the structure of the song. Then build the show by hand, record it live, or have an AI write the cue list for you.

Drum-fill aware

Rolls and snare runs mark phrase ends. The AI places strobe, stutter and flash hits on the fill's real drum hits, never as a flash on tunnel scenes.

Text as performance

A word per beat on the drop, hook words landed on their beat, and punch, shake, bounce or shatter effects. If there's no text, it hits the track title on the first drop.

Record mode

Arm record, play the track, and perform with your hotkeys, MIDI pads or the remote. Every move lands on the strip as a cue. Save it, replay it, tweak it.

Follow live

Playing the same song in the room? Trippin locks onto the live audio and fires the pre-programmed show in step with the DJ's deck.

Bring your own AI

Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint including local Ollama. Paste your key in Settings.

Your audio stays yours

No audio is ever uploaded. Only a numeric summary of the track and its title leave your machine, and only when you ask for an AI show.

MIDI mapping

Every action on a pad

Plug in any pad or key controller. Pick it, click midi next to an action, hit a pad, and it's bound. No config files, no drivers to learn.

  • Everything is mappable: scene cuts, strobe, blackout, dancer, palettes, effects, save clip, record set and more.
  • One-press learn. Re-learning a note simply steals it, so one pad is always one action.
  • Records into your show. Pad hits land on the timeline like hotkeys when you record.
  • Hot-plug friendly. Unplug and replug mid-set and it reconnects on its own.
  • Any MIDI channel, with clean press-only triggering so toggles never double-fire.

Transparent mode

Turn the scenes off and send only the dancer, glow, overlays and text with true alpha. Layer Trippin straight over your camera in OBS.

Spout is built in, with no extra DLLs on our side. In OBS, add the free Spout2 Capture plugin. NDI loads its free runtime on demand, and the app works fine without it.

Trippin Agent · new

Serato on one machine, the visuals on another

Run your DJ software on one computer and Trippin on a second. A small tray app on the DJ machine works out what's playing and sends it across your network, so the now-playing card and ticker stay right while the visuals return to OBS over NDI.

The same detection as the app

It reuses Trippin's own track detection, so Serato, rekordbox, VirtualDJ, Mixxx, the OS media session and a text file all work the same way as on a single machine.

Finds your rig by itself

It discovers Trippin on your network, or you can type a host:port. It pairs with the same PIN as the iPhone remote, reconnects on its own, and re-sends the current track when it does.

Cleans up after itself

If the agent quits or the connection drops, its track is withdrawn and Trippin falls back to its own detection. It also writes nowplaying.txt on the DJ machine for an OBS text source.

Windows and macOS. Turn on the remote in Trippin's Settings first, then download the agent.

Performance check · new

Know what your machine can run

One button in Settings steps through every heavy 3D scene and every Unity show, live, and measures how it really runs on your GPU. Anything that can't hold 30 fps drops out of rotation, so the auto-pilot stops picking it.

  • Measured, not guessed. 3D scenes by real frame time, Unity shows by the engine's own frame counter.
  • Nothing is deleted. A slow scene gets a "slow" tag and can be ticked back into rotation any time.
  • Remembered. The result is saved for your machine, and the scene picker flags the slow ones.
  • Cancel any time. Press the button again and your scene is restored.

Everything else

The whole toolkit

Auto-pilot

Cuts on phrase boundaries and drops, picks scenes by energy, and never strobes a tunnel. Or run it manually, or lock a single scene.

Beat-locked performers

Dancers, a drummer, guitarists, a keyboardist and a sax player, all on the beat, with glow and trails. Routines re-anchor to the bar so they start cleanly on a phrase.

Timeline & AI show builder

Script a full set cue by cue, or let the AI builder analyse a track and lay out scenes, text, fills and strobes for you to tweak.

Text & effects

Timeline text cues with punch, shake, strobe, bounce and shatter. Mirror and kaleidoscope effects on top of any scene.

Palettes

One global palette recolours every scene, the dancer glow and the text, so your whole set matches your brand. Or pick auto and the colours follow the mood of the music.

Hotkeys & MIDI

Every action has a key, and MIDI pad controllers can be mapped with one-click learn. Pad presses record into your timeline.
