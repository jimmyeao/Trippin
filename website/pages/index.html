How it works

It hears what you hear

No cables, no MIDI mapping, no timecode. Trippin captures your system audio, or a specific input, and works out the rest.

  • Tempo & phase. Locks to the beat and the bar, with a neural check for the downbeat.
  • Breakdowns. When the drums drop out, visuals soften and the camera slows. The drop hits hard.
  • Full control when you want it. Hotkeys, MIDI pads, an iOS remote and OSC for TouchOSC.
  • Timeline editor. Script a whole set by hand, or let the AI show builder lay out cues from your track.
