The library

Find a look, keep what you like

The control panel's library shows every scene as a thumbnail with its real name. Filter by 2D, 3D or seasonal, star your favourites, and tick scenes in or out of the auto-pilot's rotation.

  • Click to preview any scene, with the next one queued and the live output beside it.
  • Heavy 3D scenes are gated to GPUs that can run them. The performance check measures your machine and tags the slow ones.
  • Seasonal scenes (Halloween, Christmas, fireworks) only enter the playlist in season.
