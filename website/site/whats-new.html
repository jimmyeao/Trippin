What's new

The big changes since v0.8, newest first. Every release is on GitHub.

v0.15latest

Two more engine shows, and steadier tempo

  • Highlands and Coaster join the Unity engine shows. They are ports of two of the slowest GPU scenes.
  • Tempo detection no longer flaps onto a false one-and-a-half-times pattern.
  • Mac: the control and editor windows no longer pulse.
v0.14

Real names, and a performance check

  • Scenes and dancers have proper names everywhere: the panel, the pads, search and the iPhone remote. Your saved settings and timelines are unaffected.
  • Run the performance check in Settings: it measures every heavy and Unity scene on your GPU and takes the ones under 30 fps out of rotation, tagged "slow" and easy to put back.
v0.13

Trippin Agent, and a band

  • Trippin Agent, a tray app that sends what's playing to a Trippin on another machine. A new "Remote" now-playing source takes it in.
  • A band on stage: two drummers, two guitarists and a keyboardist, then a sax player in 0.13.1.
  • Retina Macs: the control and editor windows open at their real size.
v0.12

Robot shows

  • Three new Unity engine shows: an android DJ at the decks, a robot rave, and an assembly line of industrial arms, then a robot club with a crowd of robots facing the DJ.
v0.10and v0.11

21 new engine shows

  • Helix, tesseract, polyhedra, knot, gyroscope, lattice, pendulum, spectrum, eclipse, light rain, warp, LED wall, fountain, galaxy, nebula, terrain, ocean, Chladni, tunnel, orb and radar.
  • v0.11 reworked nine of them after review: helix, knot, gyroscope, pendulum, nebula, tunnel, radar, terrain and ocean.
v0.9

A smarter auto-pilot, and new looks

  • Free cuts on section changes. The auto-pilot now cuts when the music changes section, with the bar grid as a backstop instead of the rule.
  • Strobe hits on drum fills, with cuts that no longer have to sit on the bar grid, plus a new row of pads.
  • Palette "auto" matches colours to the mood of the music.
  • Comic and wire dancer looks, added to shadow, neon and strobe.
  • Audio capture heals itself. A dead or silent stream is rebuilt instead of leaving Trippin deaf.
  • Aurora now reacts across the whole sky.
